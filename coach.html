Pose Coach
——

Your second pair of eyes for portraits

Point the phone at your model. The coach reads their pose and the frame live, then tells you one thing at a time: where to move the camera, and what to tell the model.

  • 01Pick the shot: head, half body or full body.
  • 02Follow the yellow cues for the camera, the blue ones for the model.
  • 03When everything is green it takes the photo for you.

Loads a small pose model (~3 MB) on first start. Everything runs on your phone; no images leave it.
